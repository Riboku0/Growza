# Growza: Complete App Experience

[Open the separate editable Canva presentation](https://www.canva.com/d/13lKOAJdvJtGl_x)

PowerPoint: `Growza-Complete-App-Experience.pptx`

12 English slides introduce the app to a new student, then explain its implementation. Presenter notes expand the explanation without crowding the slides. Team: Ahmed Al-Amoudi and Alexander.

## Content

1. Project and team
2. The idea and student benefit
3. First subject and exam setup
4. Home and the next study action
5. Focus timer and session review
6. Per-subject garden and achievements
7. PDF headings and AI topic suggestions
8. Schedule, Blackboard and reminders
9. Adjusting a study plan when time changes
10. Language, appearance and backups
11. Flutter, SQLite, Cloudflare Worker, D1 and Workers AI
12. Implementation evidence and first-use invitation
